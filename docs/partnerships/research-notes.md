## Stationery & Office Supplies Ltd. (`sos`)

- Verified: supplying workplaces since 1965; acquired the manufacturing assets of Book Empire Limited in May 2018; locally made SEEK notebooks, quire books and writing pads; showrooms in Kingston and Montego Bay; contact form with no public email. Sources: sosjm.com/about-us, /seek, /contactus.
- The SEEK page is a product listing only. Manufacturing claims are cited to the About page.
- Not used: "number one" and "largest" (the company's own superlatives), and the Jamaica Stock Exchange listing.
- Open: the About page gives the Kingston address as both 21-25 and 23-25 Beechwood Avenue, so the deck names the street only. No community or education programme is published, and none is claimed. No named contact was verified.
- This is the stationery company, not SOS Children's Villages.

## Tank-Weld (`tankweld`)

- Verified: formed in 1970 as a steel fabrication company; scholarship programme since 2006, now more than 70 a year; has built computer and homework centres; the Fiwi Centre opened in Waterhouse in 2007. Sources: tankweld.com/giving-back/, /about/, /fiwi-centre-computer-homework-facility/.
- The 2024 donation of 15 computers is attributed to the JMMB Joan Duncan Foundation, whose article describes the centre as operated with the support of Tank-Weld Metals. Source: jmmb.com article dated 6 June 2024.
- Not used: the scholarship value ("approximately $4.5 million"), because the page does not state the currency.
- Open: no named contact was verified. The deck does not promise participation by students from Tank-Weld's community centres.

## Sandals Foundation (`sandalsfoundation`), exploratory

- Verified: education focus area including digital literacy; two-stage 2027 process, with concept applications open 6 to 27 July 2026 and full proposals by invitation only; organisations must be legally registered; projects need measurable outcomes and long-term benefit; one-off events without sustainable impact, and sponsorships or prizes without broader community benefit, are not funded; fifty tablets with Rainforest at St Mary Primary School (December 2021); Lessons Alive campaign (August 2020). Sources: sandalsfoundation.org news pages and /contact.
- The site blocks automated fetchers, so the pages were read with a browser user agent.
- The deck points to the contact page and does not print an email address.
- Open: whether follow-up mentoring or reusable school resources would count as lasting benefit, and when the next concept window opens.
